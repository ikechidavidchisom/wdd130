History

Rafting Co. began in 1998 with a simple mission: to share the thrill of whitewater rafting with the world. What started as a small group of passionate river guides has grown into a premier adventure company known for unforgettable experiences and unmatched safety standards.

Over the years, we've navigated roaring rapids, tranquil streams, and everything in between—building a legacy of trust, excitement, and connection with nature. Our team is made up of seasoned experts who live for the rush of the river and the joy of guiding others through it.

Today, Rafting Co. continues to explore new waters, expand our offerings, and inspire a new generation of adventurers. Whether you're a first-time paddler or a seasoned thrill-seeker, we invite you to be part of our story—one rapid at a time.
